# Self-check

Run this against the edited draft before returning it. Answer each item pass or fail. Fix every failure and re-check.

For detect requests, skip to the last section.

## Scope

1. Is this draft in the user's own voice? If it goes out under someone else's name, was "Give it a pulse" skipped entirely and only the pattern catalog applied?
2. If the voice owner was genuinely unclear, was it asked rather than assumed?

## Invention

This section is non-negotiable. A failure here means the edit is unusable, not merely imperfect.

1. Does the edit add zero new claims, statistics, examples, quotes, names, dates, or sources?
2. Does every specific detail in the output trace back to the original draft or to something the user supplied?
3. Were hedges left as hedges rather than upgraded into certainty the draft did not earn?
4. Where a sentence needed a number or a source to land, was the user asked instead of the gap being filled?

## Proportion and voice

1. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no aggressive compression that strips out character?
2. Are the writer's distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, admitted uncertainty, and digressions still present?
3. Were strong human sentences left alone rather than rewritten for consistency?
4. Is the draft free of the tidying reflex that makes every paragraph the same length and polish?
5. Was the writer's structure and detours preserved unless the structure was hurting the piece, and is any reorganization explained in "What changed"?
6. Is useful edge intact: strong opinion, blunt language, humor, profanity, self-interruption, honest admission?
7. Would the writer recognize this as their own voice?

## Words and phrases

1. Are banned words, high-frequency AI vocabulary, often-empty adverbs, and often-empty phrases removed except where quoted, owned by the brand, or genuinely load-bearing?
2. Are filler constructions replaced with their direct equivalents?

## Patterns

1. Are binary contrasts, negative listings, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, and rhetorical setups gone?
2. Are colon reveals rewritten as plain sentences, with sentence case after colons unless grammar, a proper noun, a title, or code requires otherwise?
3. Are trailing `-ing` clauses either replaced with a real mechanism or deleted?
4. Are importance puffery and promotional language replaced with plain facts?
5. Is weasel attribution either sourced by name or flagged to the user, with nothing invented to fill the gap?
6. Are copula avoidance and weak verb phrases replaced with is, has, and direct verbs?
7. Is synonym cycling collapsed back to the clear repeated word?
8. Is the draft in active voice with human subjects, and do no inanimate things perform human verbs?
9. Are specific facts protected rather than smoothed into generic importance?
10. Are dramatic fragmentation, rule-of-three decoration, false ranges, and robotic rhythm fixed?
11. Are fake-profound kickers deleted rather than rewritten into better metaphors?
12. Are summary-recap endings cut, so the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action?
13. Is formatting slop gone: emoji headings and bullet furniture, decorative mid-sentence bold, inline-header lists, bullets that should be prose, headers over sections under three sentences?
14. Are curly quotes and apostrophes replaced with straight ones? Leave letters that only look like punctuation, such as the ʻokina in Hawaiʻi, as they are.
15. Does the draft contain **zero** em dashes? This is a hard fail, not a judgment call. Count them. The only permitted exception is inside quoted material, where removing one would misquote the source. En dashes used as sentence punctuation fail the same check; numeric ranges are fine.

## Pulse

*Drafts in the user's own voice only.*

1. Does the draft take a position somewhere rather than only reporting neutrally? On reports, proposals and emails sent to someone who will act on them, skip this item: pass if the wording is plain and no new position was added.
2. Does sentence length actually vary?
3. Is complexity or mixed feeling acknowledged where it exists?
4. Is first person used where it fits and the format allows? On reports, proposals and emails sent to someone who will act on them, pass if the draft keeps its own person; never add one.
5. Are feelings stated specifically rather than as generic concern or enthusiasm?

## Final read

1. Was the two-question audit actually run, with the remaining tells named honestly before revising?
2. Does the draft avoid robotic symmetry, repeated sentence shapes, and stacked punchy fragments?
3. Would it sound natural read aloud to a sharp colleague?
4. Does the output include the full edited draft plus a short **What changed** section?

## Detect mode

1. Is every finding a named pattern from the catalog, with the line quoted and the fix given in a few words?
2. Is the draft left unrewritten?
3. Is there no score, grade, percentage, or claim about whether AI wrote it?
4. Does the response end by offering to edit?
